## AESTHETIC & VISUAL STYLE

### Color Palette
**Primary:** Deep navy, steel gray, aviation blue  
**Accent:** Gold/amber (cockpit lighting, Golden Prison theme)  
**Contrast:** Clean white, black for text overlays  
**Occasional:** Moldovan/romantic tones for Alexa content (warm reds, earth tones)

### Visual Guidelines
- **Photos:** High contrast, slightly desaturated, cinematic feel
- **Reels:** Fast-paced cuts for aviation, slower for business/motivation
- **Carousels:** Clean typography, consistent borders, professional design
- **Stories:** Raw, behind-the-scenes, less polished (authenticity)

### Photo Style
- **Aviation:** Epic, heroic, wide-angle, golden hour when possible
- **Business:** Candid, documentary-style, "in the moment"
- **Lifestyle:** Warm, intimate, genuine moments
- **Motivation:** Bold text overlays, minimalist backgrounds

- **Business:** "POV: You're in a VC pitch meeting," car rants, "things I wish I knew"

- Couple/family content
- Behind-the-scenes work moments

### Stories (Daily)
- Raw, unpolished updates
- Polls/questions for engagement
- Reshares of mentions/tags

- United training updates

## Key Market Trends Validating These Opportunities

1. **Micro-SaaS Boom:** Solo founders hitting $5K-$50K MRR with lean teams
2. **AI Integration Expected:** 75% of SaaS will have AI by 2026 - table stakes now
3. **Vertical SaaS Premium:** Industry-specific tools command 2-3x pricing vs. horizontal
4. **SMB Underserved:** Enterprise tools too expensive, consumer tools too simple
5. **Passive Revenue:** Modern tools allow 5-15 hrs/week maintenance post-launch

## Revenue Model Summary

All five concepts use proven SaaS models:
- **Subscription tiers:** $29-$2,999/month depending on features/users
- **Freemium/Pay-per-use:** Lower barrier to entry for adoption
- **Performance pricing:** Pay for results (churn prevention, proposal wins)
- **Add-on services:** Audit prep, consulting, premium support

**Combined portfolio potential:** $5M+ ARR by Year 2 across all five products
